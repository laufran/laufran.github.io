## Role

Instructor, July 2023.

- Taught a workshop on phylogenetic comparative methods on networks (PCM on networks) at the annual Botany Conference in Boise, ID.
- Topics included running comparative methods on phylogenetic networks in Julia, with hands-on coding exercises covering trait evolution on networks.
- Workshop materials are available at [github.com/cecileane/networkPCM-workshop](https://github.com/cecileane/networkPCM-workshop).
