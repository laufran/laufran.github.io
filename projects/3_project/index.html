The genus Calceolaria (Calceolariaceae) is a charismatic group of slipperflowers, remarkable for their radiation throughout the Andes, intriguing floral form, and oil-reward pollination system. They have long attracted the attention of botanists, but many evolutionary questions about them remained difficult to answer, as their phylogenetic history was largely unresolved until recently.

Phylogenetic backbone. Before starting my PhD, I worked as a data analyst in the Espíndola Lab extracting, aligning, and annotating chloroplast genomes across 14 Calceolaria (Calceolariaceae) species toward resolving the genus’s phylogenetic backbone and modeling its biogeographic history (Frankel et al., 2022).

Niche overlap and hybridization. I’ve continued working with Calceolaria as a postdoc in the Tribble Lab in collaboration with the Espíndola Lab, now estimating the relationship between niche overlap and hybridization histories across the genus.
