The genus *Calceolaria* (Calceolariaceae) is a charismatic group of slipperflowers, remarkable for their radiation throughout the Andes, intriguing floral form, and oil-reward pollination system. They have long attracted the attention of botanists, but many evolutionary questions about them remained difficult to answer, as their phylogenetic history was largely unresolved until recently.

**Phylogenetic backbone.** Before starting my PhD, I worked as a data analyst in the Espíndola Lab extracting, aligning, and annotating chloroplast genomes across 14 _Calceolaria_ (Calceolariaceae) species toward resolving the genus's phylogenetic backbone and modeling its biogeographic history {% cite frankel2022biogeography %}. 

**Niche overlap and hybridization.** I've continued working with _Calceolaria_ as a postdoc in the Tribble Lab in collaboration with the Espíndola Lab, now estimating the relationship between niche overlap and hybridization histories across the genus.
