Before starting my PhD, I worked as a data analyst in the Espíndola Lab extracting, aligning, and annotating chloroplast genomes across 14 *Calceolaria* (Calceolariaceae) species — Andean slipperflowers — toward resolving the genus's phylogenetic backbone and modeling its biogeographic history ({% cite frankel2022biogeography %}).

I've continued working with *Calceolaria* as a postdoc in the Tribble Lab, now estimating the relationship between niche overlap and hybridization histories across the genus.
